# AI-Powered Features

## Pathway Details Generation

In IPSA, you can access AI-generated details about pathways to enhance your understanding of the charts. Here's how to use this feature:

1. Look for the information icon (i) before each pathway name in the charts.
2. Click on this icon to open a popup window.
3. The AI will generate detailed pathway information in real-time.

![Pathway Details Icon](pathway-details-icon.png)

The AI-generated content may include:
- Pathway description
- Key components and their functions
- Related biological processes
- Potential health and disease implications

![](pathway-details-popup.png)

## AI Result Interpretation

IPSA's AI-Powered tab allows you to use Large Language Models (LLM) to interpret your analysis results.

### How to Use

1. Go to the "AI-Powered" tab in your analysis view.
2. Select the analyses you want interpreted.
3. Click "Generate Interpretation".
4. Wait for the AI to process and provide insights.

![AI Interpretation Selection](ai-interpretation-selection.png)

### Managing Interpretations

![Interpretation Table](interpretation-table.png)

Your AI-generated interpretations are listed in a table with the following columns:

1. **Dataset(s)**
2. **Created at**
3. **Action**

#### Action Buttons:

- View
- Download as PDF
- Download as DOCX
- Remove

![](view-results-interpretation.png)
## Best Practices

- Use AI interpretations to complement your expertise, not replace it.
- Validate AI-generated pathway details with scientific literature.
- Use AI insights as a starting point for further investigation.
- Save important interpretations for future reference.

By leveraging these AI-powered features, you can enhance your pathway analysis, gain deeper insights, and accelerate your research in IPSA.
